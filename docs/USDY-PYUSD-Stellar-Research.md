# Research: USDY and PYUSD on Stellar Network

## US Dollar Yield (USDY)

### What is USDY?

USDY is a yield-bearing stablecoin issued by **Ondo Finance**. It is backed by short-term U.S. Treasuries and bank demand deposits, offering approximately **5.3% APY**. It is often called a "yieldcoin" because it combines the stability of a dollar peg with earning yield.

**Important:** USDY is **not available to U.S. persons or residents** due to regulatory restrictions.

### USDY on Stellar

- **Launched:** September 2025 on Stellar
- **Use cases:** Wealth preservation, cross-border payments, treasury management, collateralization
- Stellar provides access across 180+ countries via anchors, wallets, and DEXes

### Where to Buy USDY on Stellar

1. **Stellar DEX / AMMs**
   - **Lobstr** (lobstr.co) — Swap via Stellar Classic Swaps (SDEX) or Aquarius AMM. If USDY has liquidity pools, you can swap XLM or USDC for USDY.
   - **Soroswap** (soroswap.finance) — Stellar AMM with aggregator. Swap supported Stellar assets for USDY if pools exist.

2. **Exchanges (may require bridge/withdraw to Stellar)**
   - **Bybit** — Offers USDY/USDT pair (verify if Stellar withdrawal is supported)
   - **Phemex** — Onchain Trade platform for USDY

3. **Ondo / Anchors**
   - Ondo Finance distributes USDY via ecosystem partners. Check [ondo.finance/usdy](https://ondo.finance/usdy) and [anchors.stellar.org](https://anchors.stellar.org) for Stellar-specific onramps.

## PayPal USD (PYUSD)

### What is PYUSD?

PYUSD is a stablecoin issued by **Paxos Trust Company**, regulated by the New York State Department of Financial Services. It is **fully backed 1:1** by USD deposits and U.S. Treasuries. PayPal and Venmo offer it natively with up to 4% annual rewards.

### PYUSD on Stellar

- **Launched:** September 2025 on Stellar
- **Issuer address (Mainnet):** `GDQE7IXJ4HUHV6RQHIUPRJSEZE4DRS5WY577O2FY6YQ5LVWZ7JZTU2V5`
- Built on Stellar Asset Contract (SAC), compatible with Soroban smart contracts
